Design principle

The Problem

Balancing operational speed with legal precision.

User Challenge

Fragmented Context & Low AI Trust

Legal counsel lost hours cross-referencing past amendments, emails, and clause bases. Black-box AI tools generated hallucinations and lacked verifiable sources, leading reviewers to mistrust automated summaries.

Business Challenge

Contract Bottlenecks & Exposure

High deal turnarounds caused legal backlogs, directly delaying deal velocity. Missing subtle non-standard clause variations introduced immense compliance and financial liability across enterprise portfolios.

Research + Insights + Mental Model

Lawyers knew how to review contracts. The challenge was feeling confident that nothing was missed.

Three themes surfaced consistently during research: understanding deep context, tracing source line-age, and retaining final decision authority.

User Mental Model

"We don't want AI to make legal decisions for us."

Lawyers don't seek full automation. They require high-precision retrieval, instant discrepancy highlighting, and clear audit trails so they can make the final human call faster.

AI Allocation Framework

Where AI adds value (and where it shouldn't)

⚡ What AI Handles Best

  • • Parsing unstructured long-form text instantly
  • • Highlighting non-standard clause deviations
  • • Generating inline citations & comparative diffs

🛡️ What Humans Must Retain

  • • Accepting or rejecting contractual alterations
  • • Final risk assessment for non-standard terms
  • • Approvals on liabilities and governance thresholds

Product Strategy

Designing for maximum trust and workflow velocity.

Pillar 01

Traceable Evidence

Every AI suggestion is coupled with exact line-level references and source links to original document bases.

Pillar 02

Progressive Disclosure

Avoid cognitive overload by presenting high-risk deviations first, allowing deep dives only on demand.

Pillar 03

Human Agency

AI acts strictly as a co-pilot. All legal acceptance, redlines, and submissions require direct user consent.

Robustness

Edge Cases Handled

Low Confidence Scoring

When AI confidence falls below threshold, items are flagged with "Manual Review Recommended" rather than providing vague guesswork.

Scanned/OCR Documents

Gracefully handles poor quality scans by showing image-source callouts alongside extracted text to enable immediate visual sanity checks.

Conflicting Master Terms

When an addendum contradicts a Master Services Agreement (MSA), side-by-side comparison highlights hierarchy conflicts clearly.

Governance & Safety

How We Handled Human-in-the-Loop (HITL)

1. Explicit Accept/Reject Action Controls

No AI redline or suggestion is committed to the document without active button confirmation from legal counsel.

Interactive Verification

2. Citation Bounding Box Focus

Hovering or clicking any summary auto-scrolls the native contract PDF view directly to highlighted source coordinates.

Bidirectional Traceability

3. Feedback Loop Learning

When users edit an AI suggestion, the system logs anonymized feedback to refine future fine-tuning prompts.

Impact

The value was bigger than faster drafting. The review became easier to understand and trust.

By bringing information, supporting evidence, and review tools into one workflow, we reduced manual effort however decision making is with the reviewer.

For users

  • • Find relevant information more easily
  • • Spend less effort comparing documents
  • • Understand risks with better context
  • • Keep control of final decisions

For the business

  • • More consistent review workflows
  • • Less repeated manual effort
  • • Clearer review ownership
  • • A foundation for AI-assisted legal work

What I learned

People did not want more AI. They wanted less effort and more confidence.

Designing for enterprise AI is not about automating human expertise away—it is about designing high-trust feedback loops, transparent verification states, and cognitive clarity.
