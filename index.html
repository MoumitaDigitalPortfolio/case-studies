How I work

Good enterprise design should make difficult work feel lighter, not heavier.

I start by understanding how the work happens today: the people involved, the decisions they need to make, and the places where the process becomes slow or confusing. From there, I work with the team to make the experience clearer without losing the detail people need.

Understand
