A service experience designed to feel clear, familiar, and ready when dealers need it.

The challenge

Access became difficult before the work even began.

Dealers needed essential services, but access often depended on payment processes, manual support, and systems that felt harder to use than the task itself.

Before

Too many steps before service access.

Request access Wait Follow up Manage manually

Design opportunity

Make access feel direct and predictable.

The platform needed to reduce dependency on manual processes while remaining approachable for dealers with different levels of digital experience.

Research

I started with the dealer’s day, not the dashboard.

Conversations with Maruti stakeholders helped uncover where access slowed down, what information mattered most, and which parts of the experience needed to feel more familiar.

01

Language mattered

The experience needed to feel comfortable for Hindi-speaking users.

02

Service updates came first

Dealers wanted to understand what was active and available before reviewing old transactions.

03

Reports needed less effort

Complex reporting could be simplified into clearer, task-focused views.

Product decisions

Simple choices made the system easier to use.

01

Prepaid over credit

Dealers could add balance and access services without waiting for credit approval.

02

Hindi-first access

Language support was treated as part of usability—not as an optional feature.

03

Dashboard first

Important actions and service status appeared early, reducing unnecessary navigation.

04

Reports with a purpose

Information was grouped around common questions instead of exposing every available data point.

The solution

A service journey designed like a clear route.

The experience brought the essential tasks into one simple path: add balance, choose a service, understand usage, and take action when needed.

Dashboard-first

What matters appears first.

Service status, available balance, and useful actions were brought together so dealers could understand their situation without searching across the platform.

Design principle

Show the next useful action before showing more information.

Outcome

Less time figuring out the system. More time running the business.

The redesigned marketplace gave dealers a clearer path to services while giving Maruti teams a more structured way to manage access and usage.

Access

Services became easier to reach.

Usability

Language and clarity were built into the experience.

Operations

Service activity became easier to understand and manage.

Make access simple. Keep the next action clear.

Reflection

The best simplification was not removing features.

It was organizing the experience around what dealers needed to do next.

This project reinforced that enterprise products do not need to feel complicated simply because the business behind them is complex. Clear language, familiar actions, and useful information can make a large system feel approachable.

A good dealer experience should help people get back to their work—not make the software become the work.
